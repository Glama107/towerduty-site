Tower Duty: Support

Tower and ground control at six French airports, for iPad.

Contact

Write to guillaume.mareschal@icloud.com. Please mention your iPad model and iPadOS version.

Getting started

Start with Training from the main menu: three short lessons cover a landing, a departure, and both on the same runway. The Phraseology button explains every instruction and what the crew does with it.

Is it free?

Yes, entirely: all six airports, the twelve scenarios, endless mode and the daily shift, with no in-app purchase, no subscription and no ads.

An airport or a scenario is locked. Airports open as your controller level rises: fly shifts at the airports you already have to earn experience. Each scenario opens once you have finished the one before it.

Frequently asked questions

The iPad gets hot or the game stutters. Open Settings in the game and set Graphics quality to Low. It removes the towns, shadows and depth shading.

I left the app during a shift. The shift is saved when the app goes to the background. The main menu offers to resume it.

An arrival went around although I cleared it. Crews go around when the runway is still occupied as they get close. Use "Expect late landing clearance" when the runway will be free at the last moment.

Two aircraft are stuck on the taxiway. Their strips show "Blocked". Tell one of them to give way to the other, or send one another way with the taxi map.

No voices. Check the voice toggles in the game's Settings and the iPad's silent mode. Better voices can be downloaded in iPadOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
