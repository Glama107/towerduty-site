Tower Duty: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Tower Duty is an air traffic control game for iPad. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases

Tower Duty Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment: the developer never sees your name, Apple ID, payment method or address. The game only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID owns Tower Duty Pro, and remembers the answer on your iPad so the unlocked content is available offline. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What stays on your iPad

Your career progress (level, stars, best scores, logbook, achievements), your settings and, if you leave the app during a shift, a copy of that shift so you can resume it. These are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Daily Shift score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard so you can compare it with other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from it. You can sign out of Game Center in iPadOS Settings at any time.

Voices

Radio voices are produced on the device by the iPad's built-in speech synthesis. Nothing you hear or do in the game is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Tower Duty does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
